Solar 360 was an app we built at Tuenti in 2022 to monitor energy production and consumption from solar panels.

## My role

- **Android:** I led the Android app, built with Jetpack Compose and with WebViews for some of the content.
- **iOS coordination:** informally, I helped my iOS colleague, who had less experience, organize his sprints, and I spoke on behalf of both mobile platforms.

The app was never released.
