AI assistant for teachers that runs on the interactive whiteboards in the classroom. It is deployed in hundreds of schools across the United States.

I design and build LLM-based features that run both locally, on the device itself, and remotely.

In addition:

- I analyze and optimize the performance and memory use of the app on the whiteboard: memory leaks, profiling of startup and slow frames, and work off the main thread to prevent ANRs.
- I maintain and evolve the company's web portal, built with React.
- I am part of the on-call rotation: when it's my turn, I coordinate the work and handle high-severity incidents.
